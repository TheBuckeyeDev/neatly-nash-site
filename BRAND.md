# Neatly Nash brand guide

One page on using the logo, colors, and fonts. Every file listed here is in
`static/images/brand/` and is published at `https://neatlynash.com/images/brand/<file>`.
The Canva Brand Board ("Neatly Nash Brand Board") shows the same system.

## Logos

There are two logos. Use the wordmark wherever it fits; use the monogram only where
the space is small and square.

| Logo | Use it for | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wordmark, stacked** ("neatly" over "nash") | Website header, business cards, flyers, leave-behind cards, slides | `neatly-nash-wordmark-stacked-*` |
| **Wordmark, one line** ("neatly nash") | Short, wide spaces: email signatures, invoice headers, banners | `neatly-nash-wordmark-one-line-*` |
| **Monogram** ("nn" on a shelf line) | Profile photos (Instagram, Facebook, Google), app and browser icons, stickers, Story highlight covers | `neatly-nash-monogram-*` |

Each logo comes in four versions. SVG scales to any size and is best for print;
PNG is best for apps, Canva, and social uploads.

- `-on-cream`: dark logo on a cream background
- `-on-bark`: light logo on a bark brown background
- `-transparent-for-light`: dark logo, no background, for placing on white or light photos
- `-transparent-for-dark`: light logo, no background, for placing on brown or dark photos

The monogram also comes in one color (`neatly-nash-monogram-one-color-cream` and
`-one-color-bark`, no background) for stamps, embroidery, engraving, and colored
backgrounds like tan or melon where sage would disappear.

## Fonts

- **Outfit, ExtraLight (200):** the wordmark only. Wide letter spacing (0.3em).
- **Cormorant Garamond:** headings ("Less to manage. *More to love.*").
- **Jost:** body text.

All three are free Google Fonts. The logo files are drawn shapes, so they look
right even on computers that don't have these fonts.

## Spacing and size

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around any logo at least the height of one
  "n". Don't crowd it with text or the edge of the page.
- **Smallest size:** stacked wordmark at least 120 px wide on screen or 1 inch in
  print; one-line wordmark at least 160 px or 1.25 inches. Below that, use the monogram.
- **Tiny icons:** below about 48 px, use `static/images/favicon.svg`. It has thicker
  lines so it stays readable in a browser tab.

## Don'ts

- Don't add an icon next to the wordmark (no clouds, houses, or sparkles).
- Don't stretch, squash, rotate, outline, or add shadows to either logo.
- Don't swap the colors around (for example, "nash" in melon) or put the logo in
  colors outside this palette.
- Don't put the dark logo on a dark background or the light logo on a light one;
  pick the `-for-light` or `-for-dark` file instead.
- Don't retype the wordmark in another font. Use the files.

## Voice

Warm and plain-spoken, like a friend who's good at this. First person ("I", not
"we"). Specific over salesy.
